## Purpose and scope

The next milestone is an integrated, private, representative Bibleberry experience: a complete approved synthetic month in **private Sanity staging**, safely released by the Cloudflare gateway, usable through one coherent connected reader, with correct local reader participation and Scripture Card behavior. Establish what the team means by **internal alpha** before treating local tests or a native Simulator build as qualification.

Status vocabulary: **PASS (limited)** = a specifically linked subset is demonstrated, not the whole gate; **NOT VERIFIED** = no accepted evidence of the full gate; **OWNER DECISION** = Aaron must choose the alpha boundary; **FAIL** = a demonstrated acceptance failure. Do not convert NOT VERIFIED into PASS based on a merged PR or a future-oriented description.

## Distinct qualification stages

- **Internal alpha:** Private staff/testers, approved synthetic or public-safe editorial material, one connected candidate artifact and a repeatable published-month rehearsal. The exact account and native-channel scope requires Aaron's decision.
- **Controlled beta:** Real testers, actual supported device/channel flows, hosted account recovery if included in approved beta scope, real UX/accessibility/performance results, monitored delivery and correction path. Device-specific exceptions are explicit.
- **Public launch:** Approved real content, production credential/privacy configuration, store/distribution/signing if shipping installed apps, dependable recovery/deletion/support, backup/restore, operational monitoring/incident handling, and formal owner go/no-go. The [release readiness checklist](../release/release-readiness-and-rollback.md) controls its separate proof.
